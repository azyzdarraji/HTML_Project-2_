Why parents feel lost

Parents don't understand. HTML, CSS, "function": these words mean nothing to them.

Instructors have no time. 15 personal messages after an evening class take an hour.

Kids quit. Parents lose interest, and some kids stop the course.

How it works

Three steps. A few minutes.

  1. 1

    Write notes

    For each kid: what they built, what was hard, and the next step.

  2. 2

    Draft messages

    Pick a language. The AI writes one simple message per kid, with one activity to try at home.

  3. 3

    Check and send

    You read and edit each message. Then copy it to WhatsApp.
